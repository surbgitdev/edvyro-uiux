Task 2: User Research and Accessibility Audit

Surabhi Das | XYZ University student support | UI/UX Internship

Target user

B.Tech students (years 1-3) at XYZ University, mostly on mobile, who need help with timetables, attendance, ID cards and similar services.

Interview questions

  1. Describe the last time you needed help at university. What did you do first?
  2. Where did you look for support information, and how long did it take?
  3. What made you hesitate or give up, if anything?
  4. How do you usually access university services: phone, laptop, or app?
  5. After asking for help, what would make you feel confident it was received?

Responses (3 students)

Synthesis

  • All three spent 15-60 minutes finding basic information, using informal channels (classmates, WhatsApp) first.
  • Two of three struggled to identify which department owns an issue.
  • Two of three mainly use a phone; the third uses it for quick updates.
  • All three want clear confirmation after asking; two want tracking or status updates.

Design problem and success criteria

Students cannot quickly find the right office or know whether a request was received. Success: a request submitted in under 90 seconds, correct department chosen without guessing, and a reference number plus status visible immediately.
