Revised prototype

Reflection

Testing with 3 sample participants confirmed that the reference number needed more prominence. Interviews showed that confirmation and clear ownership mattered more to students than extra features. The revised flow puts both on screen at the moment of submission. Next step: validate with real usability sessions and measure task time.
